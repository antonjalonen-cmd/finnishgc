Who we help

Four client groups with one thing in common: a gambling business someone wants to take forward.

Lotteries

Business development, new product verticals, digitalisation and more customer-centric operations. From strategy to execution, down to choosing the right partners and suppliers.

Gambling operators

New verticals such as horse betting, sports betting and lottery, plus plans for entering new markets.

Technology and content suppliers

Understanding what makes lotteries different, developing products for them and finding new lottery customers.

Other companies and organisations

Better content for gambling operators, and education about the business in live events or in writing.

What we do

01

Situation analyses

We analyse the current state of the business and give grounded recommendations on where development pays off first.

02

Strategy work

We plan the strategy work of a company or a business division and contribute content, not just process.

03

Digitalisation

We evaluate existing digital services or build a completely new environment, and coordinate the whole project, suppliers included.

04

Portfolio development

We assess the current game portfolio and bring in new verticals, targeted at the right customer segments.

05

Speaking and education

Speakers, panelists and writers for industry events, and business education for boards and key partners.
